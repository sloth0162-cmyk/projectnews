About Sloth Newz

News, without the unnecessary reading.

Sloth Newz is a news platform built to make staying informed simpler. We aggregate news from various trusted sources and turn lengthy articles into concise, readable summaries.

What is Sloth Newz?

Sloth Newz brings together news from multiple sources into one place. Instead of having to go through several websites and lengthy articles, users can find concise summaries and quickly understand what is happening.

Save Time

News articles can often be long and time-consuming to read. Sloth Newz focuses on providing the important information in a shorter and more accessible format.

Our Approach

Trusted Sources

We focus on aggregating news from reliable and trusted sources.

Concise Summaries

Long-form news is condensed into short, adequate and readable summaries.

Easy to Read

Our goal is to make it easier for users to understand the important parts of a story quickly.

We Are Still Building

Sloth Newz is currently under development. We are continuously working on improving the platform, summaries, news coverage and overall experience.

A project under

Triarc and Thinker

Sloth Newz currently operates under the broader umbrella of Triarc and Thinker as we continue developing and improving the platform.
