The Newsroom Charter & Mission

Unbiased Intelligence, Distilled for Speed and Clarity.

Sloth News Network (SNN) was founded on a singular conviction: in an era of information overload and algorithmic sensationalism, leaders, analysts, and informed citizens need rigorous, fact-checked reporting delivered without editorial bloat.

Our Journalistic Pillars

Code of Integrity

Cross-Verification

Every dispatch undergoes multi-source corroboration across international wire services, official regulatory filings, and vetted primary sources.

Rule 01 • Accuracy First

Concise Executive Briefs

We extract high-density factual signals from 3,000-word treatises into 60-second read times, respecting our readers' focus and time.

Rule 02 • Signal Over Noise

Editorial Independence

We maintain absolute commercial and political independence. Our reporting decisions are insulated from advertisers or external influence.

Rule 03 • Zero Conflicts

The Sloth Intelligence Engine

Sloth News combines professional newsroom oversight with state-of-the-art automated ingestion pipelines. Our agents scrape real-time feeds, cluster related storylines, remove duplicate syndications, classify topics across 11 key verticals, and produce neutral, fact-based abstracts.
